Biography

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Normal University. My research journey has taken me through blockchain and distributed systems security, natural language processing, large language models, multi-agent systems, and AI for education.

These experiences have gradually drawn me toward Human–Computer Interaction (HCI). I am particularly interested in how emerging AI technologies shape human behavior and social interactions. Drawing on this interdisciplinary background, I am now pursuing HCI research more deeply, combining experience in system evaluation and empirical experimentation to explore questions at the intersection of humans and AI.
